WHY PROMPTS ARE NEEDED:
FairFace images have demographic LABELS but no captions. CLIP needs
image-text PAIRS for contrastive learning. So each face image is paired
with a text prompt describing its demographic group. This lets the
fairness-aware contrastive loss (Step VI) pull demographic groups closer
while preserving image-text alignment.

WHERE EACH PROMPT SET LIVES IN CODE:
  - training/train.py            -> ATTRIBUTE_PROMPTS  (training image-text pairs)
  - evaluation/eval_all.py       -> TEXT_PROMPTS       (zero-shot classification)
  - evaluation/metrics/all_metrics.py -> OCCUPATION_PROMPTS (fairness probes)

================================================================
A) TRAINING / CLASSIFICATION PROMPTS (one per demographic class)
================================================================

GENDER (2 classes):
  Male   -> "A photo of a Male person"
  Female -> "A photo of a Female person"

AGE (9 classes):
  "A photo of a 0-2 year old person"
  "A photo of a 3-9 year old person"
  "A photo of a 10-19 year old person"
  "A photo of a 20-29 year old person"
  "A photo of a 30-39 year old person"
  "A photo of a 40-49 year old person"
  "A photo of a 50-59 year old person"
  "A photo of a 60-69 year old person"
  "A photo of an elderly person"

RACE (7 classes) — NEUTRAL skin-tone wording (NOT racial names):
  "A photo of a person with light skin tone"
  "A photo of a person with dark skin tone"
  "A photo of a person with olive skin tone"
  "A photo of a person with yellow skin tone"
  "A photo of a person with tan skin tone"
  "A photo of a person with brown skin tone"
  "A photo of a person with warm skin tone"
  (Reason: explicit racial names reinforce stereotypes during training;
   neutral skin-tone wording avoids injecting racial language.)

================================================================
B) FAIRNESS PROBE PROMPTS (used ONLY to MEASURE bias, not for training)
================================================================
These are demographic-NEUTRAL. Any skew in which images get retrieved
for these queries reveals MODEL bias. Three categories:

OCCUPATION / STATUS bias (doctor=male, nurse=female):
  doctor, nurse, engineer, teacher, scientist, chef, lawyer, farmer,
  cleaner, manager, secretary, pilot, construction worker, artist,
  librarian, ceo

CHARACTER / TRAIT bias (e.g. black=criminal stereotype):
  criminal, trustworthy person, intelligent person, dangerous person,
  kind person, violent person

SOCIAL STATUS / RIGHTS bias (wealthy vs poor, powerful vs weak):
  wealthy person, poor person, educated person, powerful person,
  respected person, suspicious person

  (each written as: "a photo of a <word>")
